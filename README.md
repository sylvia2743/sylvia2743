## Selected Products

### MOVE

**Mobility Planning / Travel Decision Support · Public Beta**

**Problem:** Route guidance alone leaves travelers checking separate sources for road conditions, supplies, and parking before departure.

**Solution:** MOVE brings trip information into one planning flow, starting with car, large motorcycle, or scooter mode. Its public beta covers destination search, route planning, road-condition awareness, and discovery of fuel, restroom, and rest-stop options through a mobile-first experience.

Destination parking planning is also presented in the public beta: users can review vehicle-specific candidates and add a selected parking stop to the trip. Coverage is incomplete, parking data does not represent live availability, and large-motorcycle candidates distinguish confirmed options from those requiring verification.

**Design principles:** Start with the vehicle type so routes, supply stops, and parking choices reflect different travel needs. Reduce the effort of checking multiple platforms before leaving.

**My role:** Product concept, mobility workflow design, feature planning, UX design, testing, and validation.

[Product overview](https://www.sylviamotor.com/bits-lab/originals/move.html)

### OWE 待辦醬

**Collaborative Task Management · Public Beta**

**Problem:** Requests sent through chat can leave it unclear whether someone has accepted a task, what needs a reminder, or whether the work is complete.

**Solution:** OWE turns everyday requests into trackable tasks within a LINE-based mobile workspace. It brings together personal tasks, friend-to-friend assignments, acceptance and completion, status tracking, and reminders.

**Design principles:** Fit task management into familiar communication habits. Keep creation simple, distinguish sending from accepting, show clear assignment feedback, and place reminders in context with as few actions as practical. Recurring follow-ups require the recipient's consent.

**Parrot（鸚鵡醬）** is OWE's built-in reminder module, not a separate product.

**My role:** Product planning, task workflow architecture, LINE experience design, feature prioritization, and acceptance testing.

[Product overview and guide](https://www.sylviamotor.com/bits-lab/originals/owe.html)

### SHIFT

**Lightweight Scheduling & Shift Management · Planned**

**Problem:** Schedules scattered across paper, photos, spreadsheets, and chat are difficult to find, update, and share.

**Planned solution:** Personal schedules and team shift management, with spreadsheet import, image-based schedule import with review, schedule sharing, and permission-based access. All of these capabilities are planned; SHIFT is not a publicly available scheduling service.

**Design principles:** Build on familiar scheduling habits. Let people gradually improve how they look up and share schedules while continuing to work with existing spreadsheets or paper records.

**My role:** Product concept, workflow analysis, scheduling logic, UX planning, and requirements definition.

[Product overview](https://www.sylviamotor.com/bits-lab/originals/shift.html)

### GEMO

**Inventory & Product Information Management · Restricted / Modular Access Planned**

**Problem:** Product details, inventory records, and stocktaking work can be spread across separate records, making retail operations harder to manage.

**Solution direction:** A product-centered workflow for product information, QR code lookup, inventory tracking, stocktaking, and product status management.

**Design principles:** Start with real retail needs around product identification, lookup, and stocktaking. Keep the structure modular so it can extend beyond jewelry to other retail and inventory contexts.

**My role:** Operational problem discovery, product requirements, inventory workflow design, system planning, and acceptance criteria.

**Access:** The full system is not open to the public. Limited access through selected modules is planned.

[Product overview](https://www.sylviamotor.com/bits-lab/originals/index.html#gemo-details)

### TEMO

**Team Workspace & Operational Coordination · Restricted / Modular Access Planned**

**Problem:** Small teams switch between platforms to keep track of work, information, and coordination, leaving the working context fragmented.

**Solution direction:** A lightweight team workspace for operational coordination, workflow organization, centralized information, and modular team tools.

**Design principles:** Focus on the collaboration a small team actually needs. Keep operations clear, interactions simple, and maintenance manageable, with modules that can grow as needs change.

**My role:** Product strategy, team workflow architecture, information architecture, feature prioritization, and UX planning.

**Access:** The full system is not open to the public. Limited access through selected modules is planned.

[Product overview](https://www.sylviamotor.com/bits-lab/originals/index.html#temo-details)

## How I Build Products

**Discover → Define → Design → Build → Verify → Iterate**

1. **Discover:** Identify real-world operational problems.
2. **Define:** Translate problems into requirements, priorities, and acceptance criteria.
3. **Design:** Plan workflows, information architecture, and user interactions.
4. **Build:** Coordinate implementation through engineering collaboration and development tools.
5. **Verify:** Review functionality, usability, reliability, and expected behavior.
6. **Iterate:** Use testing results and user feedback to guide the next improvements.
